Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CalculatorEngineerOne handles information in its current Android application and how to contact its developer.

Effective date: 19 November 2026
Developer / data controller: Kamen Paunov (Numevia), Plovdiv, Bulgaria
Contact: kamentuplovdiv@gmail.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to CalculatorEngineerOne, an Android application for mathematics and engineering calculations, diagrams, saved tasks and on-device text recognition from selected images. It does not automatically cover independently operated websites, AI assistants or other apps that you choose to open.

2. Data stored on your device

The current application can store your chosen language and interface settings, calculation history, engineering inputs and locally saved tasks in app storage on your device. This is used to operate the calculator and recall information you saved. The developer does not receive this local content automatically.

You may choose to export a task or result as a file. Any subsequent transfer of an exported file is controlled by you and the destination app or service that you select.

3. Camera, gallery and image recognition

If you choose to capture or select a picture of a problem, the application uses Android's system camera or file picker. Selected images can be processed on the device using Google's ML Kit text recognition technology. Temporary captured image files are used in the app's private cache and deleted after processing; abandoned temporary captures may be removed during later use. The present Android implementation does not upload these images to a developer-operated server.

Do not include sensitive personal information in images unless you are comfortable processing it on your device or intentionally sharing it with another service.

4. External services and user-initiated sharing

If you intentionally choose to open another application, website, or external AI service, or to share text or a file with it, that separate service may process the information you choose to provide according to its own terms and privacy policy. CalculatorEngineerOne does not control third-party apps opened at your direction.

If you send the developer an email, the developer receives your email address and the information you include, solely to respond to the request and handle related support.

5. Advertising and purchases

Current implementation: The reviewed Android source does not integrate an advertising SDK or Google Play Billing, and its embedded calculator has network requests disabled. No developer-operated advertising or payment account is connected to this build.

Planned releases: A free tier supported by advertising, a monthly ad-free subscription, and a one-time lifetime unlock are planned. These features are not active in the current implementation. Before enabling them, this policy and the application's Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated to identify the actual advertising and payment providers, the information they process, applicable legal bases and any required consent choices. Google Play may independently process purchase information under its own privacy terms.

6. Retention and deletion

Local app content remains on your device until you delete it through available app controls or clear the application's storage or uninstall the application, subject to your device's behavior. Exported files remain wherever you save them until you remove them. Email support conversations are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to answer requests and meet any applicable legal obligations.

To request deletion of information you emailed to the developer, contact kamentuplovdiv@gmail.com. The developer cannot delete copies that you independently provided to other services.

7. Security

The application is designed to keep calculator work on-device and limit its use of Android permissions. No software can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for deciding whether to share exported work or images with other applications.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of personal information held by the developer, and to object or lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. Contact the email above to make a request. For local data, you can review, export or remove it on your device where those features are available.

9. Children

The application is designed primarily for students, technicians and engineers. It is not specifically directed to children and does not intentionally request personal information from children through a developer account system.

10. Updates to this policy

The developer may update this policy when features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised and the current version will remain available at this address. Material changes affecting personal data will be communicated as required by applicable law.

11. Website hosting

The canonical version of this page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary website requests according to GitHub's privacy statement. This is separate from how the Android application processes data.

Questions about this policy or a privacy request? Email kamentuplovdiv@gmail.com.
